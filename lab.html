My process

How I approach a rationalisation

Inventory

Pull every report, package and data source from the content store and audit database into one list.

Measure

Execution history, distinct users, schedules and last-run dates show what's actually used.

Trace

Follow each report to its package, model and source tables to see overlap and downstream impact.

Decide

Agree keep / merge / retire with business owners, archive before deleting, and document why.
